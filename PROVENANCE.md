# Provenance

ProjectMoyle separates its public source repository from its private
development archive.

The private archive preserves historical reverse-engineering work,
experiments, executable-specific analysis, discarded approaches, and
other research material.

The public repository is assembled from reviewed material considered
appropriate for redistribution. Files are not automatically exported
from the private archive.

This separation is intentional:

- original game binaries and assets are not distributed;
- executable-derived dumps and substantial disassembly are not included;
- reconstructed or translated game routines are reviewed separately;
- third-party material requires known provenance and compatible licensing;
- machine-specific and private development information is removed before
  publication.

External technical references used during development are documented in
REFERENCES.md.

Third-party source components actually incorporated into the public
repository are documented in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
